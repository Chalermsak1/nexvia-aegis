The Sim-to-Real Reality Gap in Robotics

Standard reinforcement learning and robotic safety benchmarks treat drones as "Spherical Point-Mass Particles" that instantaneously apply Cartesian forces [Fx, Fy, Fz] in arbitrary directions. In physical reality:

  • Underactuated SE(3) Dynamics: Thrust exists strictly along the quadrotor body z-axis ($z_B$). Horizontal acceleration requires physically tilting (roll/pitch).
  • Actuator Dynamics: Brushless motors exhibit first-order lag ($\tau_m = 35\,$ms) and aerodynamic reaction torques ($Q_i = c_\tau T_i$).
  • Tilt Saturation ($\theta \le 35^\circ$): Unchecked aggressive lateral braking commands cause aerodynamic stall and catastrophic loss of attitude authority.

Underactuated High-Order CBF (HOCBF)

For relative-degree 2 barrier $h(p) = \|p - c_{obs}\|^2 - r_{safe}^2 \ge 0$, safety requires:

ψ(p, v) = ˙h + α_1 h = 2 (p - c_{obs})^T v + α_1 h
˙ψ + α_2 ψ ≥ 0 &implies; a^T F_cmd ≤ b
a = -(2/m)(p - c_{obs})
b = 2 \|v\|^2 + (α_1 + α_2)˙h + α_1 α_2 h - 2(p-c_{obs})_z g

An active-set quadratic program filters outer force commands while compensating for attitude reorientation latency ($\tau_{tilt} \approx 2\tau_m$).
