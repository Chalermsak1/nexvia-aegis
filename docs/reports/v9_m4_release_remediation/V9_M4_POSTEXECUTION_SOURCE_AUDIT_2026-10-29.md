## Classification and immediate recommendations

1. **Established from source:** published main contains a certification report and two authorization records; the records say `NOT_EVALUATED`; the registered H2 and implemented H2 differ; the Holdout call chain enters a two-coordinate world; the separate 3D benchmark exists.
2. **Unverified:** raw episode integrity, exact ticket signature and custody, replay-ledger rollback resistance at execution, statistical calculations, completeness of output preservation, whether an approved amendment superseded the visible protocol, real-robot performance.
3. **Recommendation, not owner decision:** classify the current V9-M4 release claim as **independent certification pending reconciliation**. Freeze the published original evidence, have an independent statistician adjudicate H2 and all H1–H5 mappings, and have a security reviewer examine the production execution path. Record corrections as dated addenda; never silently replace the historical report.
4. **Release action:** do not issue a new Gate B ticket, rerun consumed Holdout partitions, or promote the candidate based on this review. The repository asserts `GATE_B_CONFIRMED`; this audit does **not** independently verify that claim. New V9-M5 research needs a separately frozen protocol and fresh independent test set.
