\begin{abstract}
\noindent This audit resolves two foundational questions governing the pre-execution integrity of Milestone V9-M4: (1) exact provenance and verification of the frozen policy artifact certified by Calibration Round 2 (M2R2), and (2) the authoritative confidence-bound decision method for Hypothesis $H_3$ (Operational Utility \& True-Success Rate Noninferiority).

First, we resolve a clerical citation discrepancy in Report 18, which mistakenly cited the historical calibration artifact \texttt{results/v9/cal/selected\_policy.json} (Candidate \texttt{V9\_CAND\_2\_TIER2\_VERY\_STRICT}, raw SHA-256 \texttt{1c316d2d...}). We reconcile and prove that the authoritative M2R2 certified policy evaluated in Milestone V9-M3 is located strictly at \texttt{results/v9/cal\_r2/selected\_policy.json} (Candidate \texttt{V9\_CAND\_1\_TIER1\_STRICTEST}, raw SHA-256 \texttt{5a89d6061b379f1b34ab47be0deef8d63180d3e4c21d18c478bc7e451f5c84e2}, canonical payload SHA-256 \texttt{90dc5c0068bb62187415bba57fbf9cafc1f18e111ad5790b0214961490902034}). The production evaluator \texttt{experiments/v9\_evaluate\_holdout.py} defaults to this path, enforces candidate ID matching fail-closed, and strictly rejects the historical R1 artifact.

Second, we audit the authoritative decision method for Hypothesis $H_3$. In the preregistered protocol (\texttt{docs/v9\_experiment\_protocol.md} Section 3.2.4 \& Table 3.5.1), statistical power and sample size were derived from the paired Wald standard error with Student's $t$ critical value. In prior commit \texttt{f3ede86}, an ad-hoc dual-conjunction rule (\texttt{Student\_t and Newcombe}) was inadvertently implemented. A systematic grid search of 22,478 boundary configurations on $N = 1,200$ reveals that Student's $t$ Wald LCB and Newcombe Method 10 score LCB disagree in 250 configurations (1.11\%), demonstrating that they are distinct mathematical operators. We restore strict protocol fidelity in \texttt{experiments/v9\_evaluate\_holdout.py} by anchoring the confirmatory pass/fail decision strictly to the prespecified Student's $t$ Wald LCB (\texttt{lcb95\_delta\_tsr > -0.05} and $\widehat{\mathrm{TSR}}_{\mathrm{nominal}} \ge 0.94$), reporting Newcombe Method 10 as a prespecified sensitivity analysis with an explicit concurrence indicator (\texttt{sensitivity\_newcombe\_concurs}).

Three synthetic regression tests (\texttt{test\_60}, \texttt{test\_61}, \texttt{test\_62}) verify policy provenance, authoritative decision rule behavior, and boundary envelope divergence. All 62 preflight tests and all 173 full repository tests pass with zero failures. Zero real holdout execution occurred.

\section{Phase 2 --- Authoritative H3 Decision-Method Audit}

\subsection{Protocol Specification of Hypothesis H3}
In \texttt{docs/v9\_experiment\_protocol.md}, Hypothesis $H_3$ assesses operational utility and noninferiority of True-Success Rate (TSR) against the primary baseline V8.1 Martingale across the $S_{10}$ scenario domain ($N = 1,200$ matched pairs).

The confirmatory acceptance criteria defined in Table 3.5.1 are:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{LCB}_{95}(\Delta\mathrm{TSR}) > -0.05 \quad \text{AND} \quad \widehat{\mathrm{TSR}}_{\mathrm{nominal}} \ge 0.94
\end{equation}
Section 3.2.4 derives power from the paired Wald standard error:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{SE}_0 = \sqrt{\frac{q}{N}}, \quad q = \frac{n_{10} + n_{01}}{N}
\end{equation}
with the 1-sided 95\% Student's $t$ critical threshold:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{LCB}_{\text{Student-}t} = \overline{d} - t_{0.95, N-1} \frac{s_d}{\sqrt{N}}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Decision Rule Decoupling}
In commit \texttt{f3ede86}, \texttt{evaluate\_scenario\_holdout} was implemented as:
\begin{verbatim}
h3_v8_passed = bool(h3_v8_lcb95 > -0.05 and h3_v8_newcombe_lcb95 > -0.05 and nominal_tsr_hat >= 0.94)
\end{verbatim}
This conjunction imposed an unprespecified dual test. We updated \texttt{experiments/v9\_evaluate\_holdout.py} to anchor the confirmatory pass/fail decision strictly to the registered Student's $t$ Wald LCB:
\begin{verbatim}
h3_v8_passed = bool(h3_v8_lcb95 > -0.05 and nominal_tsr_hat >= 0.94)
h3_v8_newcombe_concurs = bool(h3_v8_newcombe_lcb95 > -0.05) if not math.isnan(...) else False
\end{verbatim}
The production evaluator now reports both metrics transparently without allowing post-hoc method substitution.

\section{Phase 4 --- Common Random Numbers (CRN) Claim Boundaries}

We reaffirm the four structural and statistical boundaries governing CRN:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Episode-Level Matched Pairing:} Matching occurs strictly at the episode level via identical integer seeds.
    \item \textbf{Common Initial Conditions:} At $t = 0$, both controllers observe bit-identical initial states and draw from identical disturbance seeds.
    \item \textbf{Mutable Stream Independence:} Each controller operates on an independent PRNG state. When a controller invokes additional sensor queries, its stream advances, inducing intentional decorrelation for subsequent draws.
    \item \textbf{Empirical Covariance Requirement:} Variance reduction $\operatorname{Var}(\overline{\Delta}) < \operatorname{Var}_{\text{unpaired}}$ mathematically requires positive covariance $\operatorname{Cov}(Y_\pi, Y_{\text{base}}) > 0$. On historical benchmark data ($N = 500$), empirical correlation was $\rho \approx 0.593$ (yielding 36.2\% SE reduction). On unexecuted holdout data, variance reduction remains an empirical property to be measured post-authorization.
\end{enumerate}
